The method

Nine steps. Three quality gates.

Every stage produces a named, dated artifact — no invisible black boxes. A citation auditor, a human sign-off on every claim, and a 34-pattern audit stand between the draft and your inbox.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Kickoff brief written in your language and signed off. No brief, no start.

  2. 02

    Pull

    Regulatory, trial registries, journals, conferences, filings, patents. Confirmed vs inferred, labelled.

  3. 03

    Evidence

    Every claim resolves to a public URL or named document, with a capture date.

  4. 04

    Analyse

    Trial-level detail: N, endpoints, effect sizes with confidence intervals, grade 3+ safety.

  5. 05

    Synthesise

    What it means for your positioning. Human judgement — not automation.

  6. 06

    Audit

    A 34-pattern AI-slop check removes hedging, padding, and unsourced assertions.

  7. 07

    Citation check

    A citation auditor re-verifies every material claim. Unresolved claims get removed.

  8. 08

    Bundle

    Dossier, battle cards, one-pager, source list — structured for a 10-minute read.

  9. 09

    Ship

    A named, dated package — typically 3–7 days from brief sign-off.

Public sources only.

Regulatory filings. Trial registries. Peer-reviewed publications. Conference abstracts. Company disclosures. Patent filings. Verified trade press. If it requires deception, a confidentiality breach, or non-public information, it is not competitive intelligence — and it is not work I take.

This is not merely a legal posture. It is the reason a compliance team can approve this vendor without a second meeting.
